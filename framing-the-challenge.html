Step 1: Review the scenario

A launch date is set. The skilling plan isn’t.

Microsoft will introduce a new AI-assisted customer support capability in eight weeks. Global Skilling must recommend how to prepare the people who will use, support, and govern it. Some required skills are covered by existing courses; other resources are in development; and important needs may remain unmet.

Your team needs to decide what content to reuse, what to build, and what to curate; and justify your plan.

Step 2: Group discussion

Start with the problem.

Today, all our course content lives in a spreadsheet. Use these questions to turn the limitations of that process into requirements for an AI solution.

  • What is broken in our current spreadsheet-based process?
  • Who uses the spreadsheet, and what are they trying to accomplish?
  • What information should the agent use, and which data is missing, inconsistent, or unreliable?
  • What rules should the agent follow when recommending what to reuse, build, or curate?
  • What should the agent produce, and what evidence should support its recommendations?
  • Which decisions require human review, and what would make the agent’s output trustworthy?

Step 3: Plan a prototype

The solution: The Skilling Needs Advisor

To solve for this scenario, you’ll build an AI-supported experience that analyzes the business need, reviews available content, identifies meaningful gaps, and recommends the appropriate skilling response.
