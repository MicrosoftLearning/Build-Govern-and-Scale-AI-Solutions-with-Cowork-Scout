Build activity

Build a skilling recommendation app

Choose Cowork or Scout, add the provided Excel catalog, and build a first version you can test.

Choose Cowork or Scout

People at the same table can use different tools. You will compare what each approach produces.

Cowork path

Give Cowork the outcome and supporting context

1Start the work

Open Cowork and begin a new task for the Skilling Needs Advisor.

2Add the Excel catalog

Download the course catalog, then add it to your Cowork task.

Download the Excel catalog
3Use the prompt to build your app

Copy the prompt into Cowork and review the plan it proposes.

Build a reusable app called the Skilling Recommendation Advisor. Do not simply answer one skilling request.

The app should:

1. Ask the user for the business outcome, target audiences, role-based tasks, desired proficiency, timing, and delivery needs.
2. Ask clarifying questions when required information is missing.
3. Use the uploaded Excel course catalog to find relevant existing content.
4. Identify meaningful content gaps.
5. Recommend whether the team should Reuse, Curate, Adapt, Partner, or Build.
6. Present the result as a structured skilling plan organized by audience.
7. Clearly identify missing information and never invent catalog offerings.

Create the reusable app experience, instructions, and workflow first. When it is ready, prompt the user to enter a skilling request so the app can be tested.

4Steer the result

Answer clarifying questions, inspect the evidence, and correct assumptions.

5Test your app

Create a test request that fits the app you built.

Use details like these as ingredients for your own scenario:

  • Product or capability: Microsoft 365 Copilot in Word and Teams, Dynamics 365 Sales, or GitHub Copilot.
  • Audience: Account executives and sales managers, software developers and engineering managers, or HR business partners.
  • Business outcome: Reduce sales-call preparation time by 20%, reach 70% weekly active use within 90 days, or shorten pull-request review time.
  • Responsibilities: Sellers prepare customer briefs, managers coach responsible use, or developers generate tests and review AI-assisted code.
  • Timing: Pilot begins December 7, 2026; production launch is January 18, 2027; teams must be ready two weeks before launch.
  • Desired output: A role-based learning plan, recommended catalog courses, identified content gaps, and a delivery sequence.
  1. Inspect what the app inferred, asked, recommended, and missed.
  2. Make one improvement before comparing results at your table.
