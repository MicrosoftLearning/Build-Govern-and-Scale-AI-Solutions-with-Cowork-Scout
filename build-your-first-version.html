Step 1: Meet the challenge

Someone sent you a spreadsheet. Build a better way.

A new skilling request has landed. Your team currently spends about 20 hours navigating an Excel catalog, finding relevant content, identifying gaps, and deciding what to recommend.

Use Cowork or Scout to build an app that turns the request into an evidence-based skilling recommendation.

Before you build

Give the advisor a simple job

Keep the first version focused on one team and one familiar workflow.

  1. 1
    Define the need

    Clarify the request, audience, outcome, and timing.

  2. 2
    Find content

    Search the Excel catalog for relevant offerings.

  3. 3
    Assess fit and gaps

    Identify what is covered, partial, unknown, or missing.

  4. 4
    Recommend a response

    Recommend what to reuse, curate, adapt, partner on, or build.

Step 2: Build an app

Choose Cowork or Scout

People at the same table can use different tools. You will compare what each approach produces.

Cowork path

Give Cowork the outcome and supporting context

1Start the work

Open Cowork and begin a new task for the Skilling Needs Advisor.

2Add the Excel catalog

Use the course catalog file provided by your facilitator.

3Use the starter prompt

Copy the prompt into Cowork and review the plan it proposes.

You are a Skilling Needs Advisor. Help our team turn a business request into an evidence-based skilling recommendation.

First, clarify the business outcome, audiences, role-based tasks, desired proficiency, timing, and delivery needs.

Then use the provided Excel catalog to identify relevant content, assess coverage as Full, Partial, or Unknown, and identify meaningful gaps. Recommend whether to Reuse, Curate, Adapt, Partner, or Build.

For every recommendation, cite the supporting catalog evidence, state assumptions, and flag decisions that require human review. Do not invent catalog offerings or treat missing information as confirmed.

4Steer the result

Answer clarifying questions, inspect the evidence, and correct assumptions.

Scout path

Give Scout the question and source to investigate

1Start the investigation

Open Scout and begin a new exploration for the Skilling Needs Advisor.

2Add the Excel catalog

Use the course catalog file provided by your facilitator.

3Use the starter prompt

Copy the prompt into Scout and inspect how it organizes the analysis.

You are a Skilling Needs Advisor. Help our team turn a business request into an evidence-based skilling recommendation.

First, clarify the business outcome, audiences, role-based tasks, desired proficiency, timing, and delivery needs.

Then use the provided Excel catalog to identify relevant content, assess coverage as Full, Partial, or Unknown, and identify meaningful gaps. Recommend whether to Reuse, Curate, Adapt, Partner, or Build.

For every recommendation, cite the supporting catalog evidence, state assumptions, and flag decisions that require human review. Do not invent catalog offerings or treat missing information as confirmed.

4Refine the recommendation

Probe missing evidence, challenge assumptions, and improve the response.

Step 3: Test and compare

Put your app to work

Test request: A product team needs skilling for a new AI-assisted customer support capability launching in eight weeks. Multiple roles need different outcomes, proficiency levels, and delivery approaches.

  1. Run the request through your solution.
  2. Inspect what it inferred, asked, recommended, and missed.
  3. Make one improvement, then compare Cowork and Scout at your table.

Table discussion

Same need. Different approaches.

  • What did each tool do well?
  • What refinement did each solution need?
  • What did both leave to human judgment?
