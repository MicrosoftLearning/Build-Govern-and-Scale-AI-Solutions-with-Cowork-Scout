Build activity

Build a skilling recommendation app

Choose Cowork or Scout, add the provided Excel catalog, and build a first version you can test.

Choose Cowork or Scout

People at the same table can use different tools. You will compare what each approach produces.

Cowork path

Give Cowork the outcome and supporting context

1Start the work

Open Cowork and begin a new task for the Skilling Needs Advisor.

2Add the Excel catalog

Download the course catalog, then add it to your Cowork task.

Download the Excel catalog
3Use the starter prompt

Copy the prompt into Cowork and review the plan it proposes.

You are a Skilling Needs Advisor. Help our team turn a business request into a skilling recommendation.

First, clarify the business outcome, audiences, role-based tasks, desired proficiency, timing, and delivery needs.

Then use the provided Excel catalog to identify relevant content and meaningful gaps. Recommend whether to Reuse, Curate, Adapt, Partner, or Build.

Do not invent catalog offerings. Clearly identify information that is missing.

4Steer the result

Answer clarifying questions, inspect the evidence, and correct assumptions.

5Test your app

Try entering: “A product team is launching an AI-assisted customer support capability in eight weeks. Create a skilling plan for support agents, team leads, and content authors. Recommend existing courses, identify gaps, and flag what still needs human review.”

  1. Inspect what the app inferred, asked, recommended, and missed.
  2. Make one improvement before comparing results at your table.
