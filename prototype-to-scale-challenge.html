The prototype looks promising. Leadership wants the team to use it for every new skilling request. What must be resolved first?

Step 1: Assess readiness

What must be resolved?

Assess the prototype through four lenses. Choose Ready, Needs work, or Unknown for each.

Value

Does the prototype address a shared problem and produce a useful outcome?

Trust and risk

Are sources, assumptions, permissions, and human-review points clear?

Ownership

Is someone accountable for the source, prompt, updates, support, and results?

Reach and adoption

Can the intended audience access, understand, and consistently use the experience?

Assess all four lenses to continue.
