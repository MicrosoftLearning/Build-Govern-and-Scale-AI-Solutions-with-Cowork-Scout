Step 2: Compare scaling paths

Match the path to the operating need

Explore each option. Consider the intended reach, required controls, integration complexity, and who will own the experience.

Path 1

Continue prototyping

Keep testing before expanding access or depending on the result.

Best fit when
Value is still uncertain or important trust, evidence, and ownership gaps remain.
Resolve next
Improve the source, prompt, safeguards, or success criteria and test again.

Path 2

Team-managed use

Share the experience with a controlled group and manage it through clear operating practices.

Best fit when
The need is shared, reach is limited, and a team can own updates, support, and human review.
Resolve next
Define the owner, approved source, access boundaries, feedback loop, and measures of value.

Path 3

Low-code solution

Turn the prototype into a repeatable, governed workflow with managed connections and makers.

Best fit when
The workflow is understood, integrations are manageable, and the organization can govern makers and environments.
Resolve next
Confirm platform fit, data connections, lifecycle management, permissions, monitoring, and support.

Path 4

Pro-code solution

Engineer a managed solution for complex integrations, broad reach, or rigorous reliability and control requirements.

Best fit when
The solution is business-critical, integration-heavy, high-volume, or requires custom security and reliability controls.
Resolve next
Establish product ownership, architecture, evaluation, deployment, observability, security, and support.
