Build the prototype

Use Cowork or Scout to build a testable first version of the Skilling Needs Advisor.

Workshop path Choose a tool to begin Change table or tool

Step 1: Review the blueprint

Review the advisor workflow

These four stages define what your prototype needs to do and produce: an evidence-based recommendation that identifies supporting content, states assumptions, and flags decisions requiring human review.

Discuss at your table: Does this workflow fit the requirements you identified?

Step 1 of 4

Define the need

Clarify the business problem, audience, desired outcomes, and timing.

Step 2 of 4

Find relevant content

Search the catalog for existing and planned content that could meet the need.

Step 3 of 4

Assess fit and gaps

Evaluate how well the content meets the need and identify important gaps.

Step 4 of 4

Recommend a response

Use the evidence to recommend what to reuse, curate, adapt, partner on, or build.

3Use the starter prompt

Copy the prompt into Cowork and review the plan it proposes.

You are a Skilling Needs Advisor. Help Global Skilling turn a business request into an evidence-based skilling recommendation.

First, clarify:
- the business outcome;
- the audiences, role-based tasks, and desired proficiency;
- timing and delivery needs.

Then use the provided catalog to identify existing or planned content, assess fit and coverage as Full, Partial, or Unknown, and identify meaningful gaps. Recommend whether to Reuse, Curate, Adapt, Partner, or Build.

For every recommendation, cite the supporting catalog evidence, state assumptions, and flag decisions that require human review. Do not invent catalog offerings or treat missing information as confirmed.

4Steer the result

Answer clarifying questions, inspect the evidence, and correct assumptions.

Step 4: Debrief

Compare the two approaches at your table

Discuss how Cowork and Scout handled the same need. The goal is not to choose a winner, but to understand how each approached the work.

  • What did Scout do well? What did Cowork do well?
  • What did one solution accomplish that the other did not?
  • What kind of refinement did each solution need?
  • What did both solutions miss or leave to human judgment?

Capture the most important difference your table observed before moving on.
